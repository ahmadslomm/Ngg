## Currencies in the rebuild

From `prisma/schema.prisma` — `Wallet` carries `coins`, `coinsTotal`, `vipCoins`, `beans`,
`balance`, plus a `version` column for optimistic locking. `WalletLedger` records
`currency` (0 coins · 1 beans · 2 vipCoins · 3 balance), `delta`, `balanceAfter`, `reason`,
`refType`/`refId`, and a unique `idempotencyKey`.
